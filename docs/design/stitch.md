## How it was run (and what to know before re-running)

- **Upload.** The full `DESIGN.md` (40 KB) is about 54k tokens once base64-encoded, too large for a single MCP tool call, so a condensed copy (10.6 KB) was uploaded: the same front matter (Material roles plus the semantic colour keys, which Stitch accepted) and every section compressed to its rules and both themes' hex values. Stitch then rewrote parts of its own design system: `primary #000000`, radii md 12 / lg 16 / xl 24, a 12px `label`, and a style guide that calls chips "pill-like". That rewrite is where the pill chips come from; `update_design_system` can correct it in a later pass.
- **Timeouts.** MCP calls give up after about 60 s. With the default model, long generations timed out and never showed up in `list_screens` or `get_project`, so orphan screens from those attempts may exist in the project. All kept screens used `modelId: GEMINI_3_5_FLASH_LITE`, and variants were generated one per call (`variantCount: 1`, three calls).
- **`edit_screens` creates a new screen** and leaves the original intact, which is why each fix pass and each dark render has its own id.
- **Tab bar.** Shell screens use **Month · Activity · + · More**, as the WP 0.3 brief asked. `DESIGN.md` (Layout & Spacing, Tab bar) still specifies five slots: Mes · Movimientos · + · Importar · Ajustes. One of the two needs to change.

## What Stitch got wrong (ignore these parts of the mocks)

1. Icons are Material Symbols loaded from Google Fonts, and the HTML pulls Tailwind from a CDN. Use Lucide (20px, 1.75 stroke) and the project's own Tailwind tokens, and never copy the HTML.
2. Chips render as grey-filled pills, and category tiles and value chips are filled instead of outlined. `DESIGN.md` specifies radius 8, `surface-1` with 1px `line-strong` (chips) or 1px `line` (tiles). Several mocks also tint the active tab with an `accent-soft` pill; `DESIGN.md` colours only the icon and label.
3. Month: the category bars stayed ink-black (white in dark) even after a fix pass. `DESIGN.md` wants 12px `mark-neutral` bars with "Other" in `mark-muted`. The insight copy is invented ("home-cooked meal initiative"), and amounts use a monospace face where `DESIGN.md` wants Inter with `tabular-nums`.
4. Transactions: rows wrap to two or three lines and the minus sign breaks away from some amounts, even after a fix pass. Build strict 56px single-line rows per `DESIGN.md`.
5. Import review: every row is drawn as a card, and a "Cancel" action was added. Only "Review" rows get `surface-1` with a hairline border; Auto and New rows sit flat in the list.
6. Quick add: the dark render lost the 16px side gutters, and both themes leave a large empty gap above the keypad. Follow the `DESIGN.md` skeleton and spacing.
7. Stitch inserted real-looking names ("Sarah", "Alex") and a stock avatar photo in drafts. The chosen mocks are fixed; check for this whenever screens are regenerated.
